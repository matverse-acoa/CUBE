# CUBE Projection

The CUBE is the geometric and navigable projection of a MatVerse organism. It is not the organism itself, the source of truth, or an authorization engine.

## Semantics

- **Center:** common state and identity coherence.
- **Faces:** declared perspectives or stages of a process.
- **Edges:** interfaces and transitions.
- **Mini-cubes:** localized skills and capabilities.
- **Color/state:** observed or declared maturity, never an automatic claim of production readiness.

## Control boundary

The CUBE sends human intent to the Cassandra Control Plane through its `/api/cassandra` proxy. The CUBE does not choose an authority class, change policy, authorize execution, or fabricate evidence.

## Valid projection

A projection is valid when it retains a link to the relevant run, kernel version, policy version, decision, parent relation, and receipt hash.
